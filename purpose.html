Our Advocacy
CCCA Education Foundation
Perserving the Legacy

The mission of the CCCA Education Foundation is to promote educational efforts pertaining to the study, research and dissemination of information related to Full Classic® automobiles for the public at large and to otherwise preserve and advance the legacy of these extraordinary automobiles through publications and programming for the enduring benefit of future generations.








Our Purpose
The Classic Car Club of America, Inc., a non-profit membership corporation chartered in the State of New York, is established with the following purposes and goals:

Facilitating the development, publication, and exchange of technical, historical, and other relevant information among members and enthusiasts interested in fine or distinctive foreign or domestic motorcars manufactured between the years 1915 and 1948.

Fostering social interaction and camaraderie among members, creating opportunities for networking, collaboration, and friendship within the classic car community.

Serving as a repository of knowledge and resources to support the maintenance, restoration, and preservation of classic cars of this era, safeguarding their heritage and ensuring their continued enjoyment for generations to come.
